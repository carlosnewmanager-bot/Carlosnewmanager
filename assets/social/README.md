# Social platform marks

Local assets for the footer links. Downloaded 2026-11-12. Brand marks identify the destination platforms; they do not imply endorsement.

- `facebook.svg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/facebook.svg
- `instagram.svg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/instagram.svg
- `threads.svg`: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/threads.svg
- These three unmodified monochrome SVGs come from Simple Icons (https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), distributed under CC0-1.0. Brand trademark rights remain with their owners.
- `vocus.png`: https://vocus.cc/static/img/icon-180x180.png — the platform's own published app/site icon, preserved in its original colours.

The icons are stored locally so footer rendering does not depend on third-party image requests. They are shown proportionally beside the existing visible names, with empty alt text to avoid repeating those names for screen readers.
